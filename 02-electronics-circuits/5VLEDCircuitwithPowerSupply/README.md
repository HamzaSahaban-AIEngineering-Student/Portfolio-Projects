# Simple LED Circuit
An electronic circuit using a DC Power Supply to power a Red LED safely through a current-limiting resistor, simulated on Tinkercad.

## Circuit Details
* **Power Supply:** 5V DC
* **Resistor:** 330 Ω
* **LED:** Red
* **Measured Current:** ~9.16 mA

## 📐 Circuit Analysis
* **Voltage ($V$):** 5V
* **LED Forward Voltage ($V_f$):** ~2V
* **Resistor Value ($R$):** 330 Ω
* **Expected Current ($I$):** 
  $$I = \frac{V - V_f}{R} = \frac{5 - 2}{330} \approx 9.09\text{ mA}$$
* **Measured Current on Power Supply:** ~9.16 mA
