# 5V LED Circuit with Diode (Forward Bias)

A simple electronic circuit built on Tinkercad featuring a Red LED powered by a 5V DC Power Supply, connected in series with a Rectifier Diode (1N4007) in Forward Bias mode and a current-limiting resistor.

## 📸 Circuit Overview
* **Power Supply:** 5V DC
* **Diode:** 1N4007 Standard Rectifier Diode (Forward Bias)
* **Resistor:** 330 Ω (Current-limiting)
* **Output:** Red LED
* **Measured Current:** ~7.45 mA

## 🛠️ How It Works
1. The diode is connected in **Forward Bias** mode (Anode to 5V Positive Rail, Cathode toward the circuit).
2. The diode conducts current with a standard forward voltage drop of `~0.7V`, delivering roughly `4.3V` to the remainder of the circuit.
3. Current passes through the 330 Ω resistor and drives the Red LED safely, consuming a measured current of `~7.45 mA`.
