Zero-SaaS Data Sovereignty

PRIVACY POLICY &
DATA PROTECTION STANDARDS

Root Rob operates under strict privacy principles. We engineer self-hosted, private infrastructures ensuring zero unauthorized data extraction, resale, or third-party tracking.

LAST UPDATED: AUGUST 2026

[ POLICY // 01 ]

1. Self-Hosted Infrastructure & Absolute Data Sovereignty

Unlike conventional cloud agencies that store your sensitive operational data on multi-tenant SaaS platforms, Root Rob deploys dedicated VPS and Dockerized container environments. Your client databases (PostgreSQL, NocoDB), AI knowledge vectors, and messaging logs remain isolated under your direct ownership and control.

[ POLICY // 02 ]

2. Information We Collect & Intake Processing

We collect only the essential information submitted voluntarily through our intake form or direct email communications:

  • • Contact Identity: Name, official email address, phone number / WhatsApp handle.
  • • Project Scope Parameters: Primary service selection, target database types, and estimated conversation volume.
  • • Technical Specifications: Workflow descriptions and hosting requirements.
[ POLICY // 03 ]

3. Form Transmission & Third-Party Service Handlers

Inbound form submissions on this website are transmitted via secure HTTPS AJAX calls to endpoint proxies (FormSubmit API or dedicated self-hosted n8n Webhooks). These handlers route payload contents directly to our official email (h.shaban2007@hotmail.com) or internal database nodes. We do not sell, lease, or monetize your contact payload to ad networks or data brokers.

[ POLICY // 04 ]

4. Infrastructure Security & Network Hardening

All web traffic to Root Rob domains is encrypted end-to-end via TLS/SSL certificates and filtered through Cloudflare edge protection. Self-hosted production nodes utilize Nginx Proxy Manager SSL routing, strict firewall rules, and containerized Docker isolation to mitigate unauthorized network intrusion and data tampering.
